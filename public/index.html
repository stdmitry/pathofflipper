Checking data…

Historical screening, not live quotes. Low and high are the cheapest and dearest prices paid in past trades, not prices you can buy or sell at now, and the gap between them is not a spread you can capture. The rank orders markets by that gap relative to the low price, times the amount traded per hour, times what a flip earns per 1,000 gold. It points to markets worth a closer look, not to proven profit.
