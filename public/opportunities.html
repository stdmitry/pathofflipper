Historical screening, not live quotes. Flips and trades use the cheapest and dearest prices paid in past trades, so their profit is an upper bound, not a spread you can capture. Runegraft conversions use an expected value: an average over many conversions, and any single conversion can lose. The ranking multiplies profit per cycle by how many cycles the market traded per hour, which is a ceiling, not an income forecast.
